Horror Movies

The horror movie genre is a fear-filled experience full of guts, ghosts, and girls.

There are many reasons as to why the horror genre can be enjoyed (beyond the spectacle of it all.) Horror shows the darkest and most human parts of people (LITERALLY! LIKE GUTS!) which can be a scary truth to see. Other times, it is just full of jump-scares that force the body to react physically.

Why watch horror? While most think it is gross, sexual, and repulsive, THEY ARE RIGHT! Horror is extreme and bloody to force people to think about their mortality, morals, and real world situations. In this website, I will be discussing the general summaries of horror films compared to their deep, philosophical meanings.

Stay and read if you are a true horror fan!
